# Analysis Framework

## Required sequence
1. Define the question and time horizon.
2. Gather current primary company sources and relevant market data.
3. Describe business model, economics, management, and key drivers.
4. Analyse financial history and forward-relevant changes.
5. Identify the initial thesis and actively search for disconfirming evidence.
6. Value the business and test scenarios.
7. Assess risks, counter-thesis, and decision implications.
8. Produce a traceable research note for human review.

## Evidence labels
- **Fact:** sourced, verifiable statement.
- **Assumption:** input selected without proof.
- **Estimate:** calculated or forecast quantity.
- **Interpretation:** reasoned judgment.

Cite sources and dates for material claims. Prefer current filings, earnings materials, investor presentations, and company disclosures. Separate what is known from what is inferred.
