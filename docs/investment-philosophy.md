# Investment Philosophy

The default stance is long-term, quality-aware value investing: seek understandable businesses, durable economics, capable capital allocation, and a sufficient margin of safety.

- Treat price and value as separate questions.
- Prefer a reasoned expected return over narrative momentum.
- Use a multi-year horizon unless the thesis is explicitly event-driven.
- Size positions with downside, uncertainty, concentration, and liquidity in mind.
- Avoid leverage unless the human investor explicitly authorizes it.
- Do not infer a buy conclusion merely because a company is high quality or cheap.

Every thesis must include the strongest credible reason it may be wrong.
