# Risk Framework

Assess the risk of permanent capital loss, not merely price volatility.

## Categories
- Business model and competitive risk
- Financial leverage, refinancing, liquidity, and dilution
- Management, governance, incentives, and capital allocation
- Cyclicality, customer concentration, and supply-chain exposure
- Regulation, technology change, legal exposure, and accounting quality
- Valuation and expectation risk
- Portfolio concentration and correlation

## Counter-thesis
For each material thesis, state:
1. What would disprove it?
2. Which evidence already contradicts it?
3. What downside scenario is plausible?
4. What metrics or events would trigger a review?

Distinguish probability from impact. Unknowns remain explicit rather than being converted into false confidence.
