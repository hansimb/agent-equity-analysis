# Valuation Framework

Valuation is a range, not a precise answer. Select methods that fit the business and explain why.

## Core methods
- Earnings or free-cash-flow multiple versus history, peers, and normalized economics.
- Discounted cash flow when cash-flow drivers can be stated credibly.
- Asset or NAV approaches for asset-heavy businesses.
- Sum-of-the-parts where segments differ materially.

## Minimum output
- Bull, base, and bear scenarios when useful.
- Key operating, margin, capital-intensity, tax, and discount-rate assumptions.
- Implied value range, current price, expected return, and time horizon.
- Sensitivity to the assumptions that matter most.

Do not hide uncertainty behind excessive precision. Reconcile valuation with balance-sheet risk and dilution.
