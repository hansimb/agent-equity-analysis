# Project Plan

## Purpose
Maintain a compact, Git-traceable context base for AI agents assisting human equity analysis. It is not an investing application or an automated trading system.

## Architecture
- `docs/`: stable methods and operating rules.
- `context/`: portfolio, market, macro, and investor context.
- `companies/`: durable company notes and source-led research.
- `data/`: raw or structured inputs.
- `research/`: dated outputs and thesis revisions.

## Workflow
1. Gather current market and company data.
2. Research the business and competing explanations.
3. Analyse financial quality and drivers.
4. Value the company with explicit assumptions.
5. Test risks and the counter-thesis.
6. State an investment thesis and uncertainty.
7. Submit for human review and decision.

## Principles
Use primary sources where possible; label facts, assumptions, estimates, and interpretations; seek disconfirming evidence; retain uncertainty; and preserve material work in Git.

## Future Development
Add lightweight templates, per-company folders, structured data conventions, source registries, and optional agent tooling only when a recurring need justifies them.
